    Does holding longer pay?

    We took every month since 2013 as a starting point and checked how each holding period ended.

    How to read itEach ring is the share of periods that made money. The bar under it shows the yearly return: the white mark is the typical case, the coloured band runs from a poor outcome to a good one.

    Poor and good outcomes are the 10th and 90th percentiles. These are past results from a mostly rising market, not a promise.

    Which model predicts better?

    Five models tried to predict each fund's return for the following month, tested on years they had never seen (2019 to 2026).

    How to read itA model is only useful if it beats the simple baseline, which just predicts each fund's past average. A lower error is better.

    Predicting next quarter's risk band

    These models predict whether a fund will be in the calmest, middle or bumpiest third of its peers next quarter. A random guess would be right about 33% of the time.
